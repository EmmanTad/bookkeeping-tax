About us

Accountants who explain, not just file

We started with a simple observation: too many Filipino business owners learn about their taxes only when a penalty notice arrives. We exist to make that impossible.

Our story

Fifteen years of clean books and calm filing seasons

Bookkeeping and Tax Services was founded by licensed CPAs who spent years in audit and industry before deciding that small and growing Philippine businesses deserved the same quality of financial care that large corporations enjoy.

Today we serve over 120 clients — freelancers, professionals, online sellers, restaurants, trading businesses and corporations — across Metro Manila and beyond. Some came to us with years of backlog; others, on day one of a new business. All of them share one thing: they never want to think twice about whether their taxes are done.

Every account is supervised by a CPA, every month closes on schedule, and every BIR deadline on our calendar is tracked — with reminders sent to you long before payment day.

What we stand for

Four values behind every entry we make

Accuracy first

Every entry is reconciled and reviewed before it reaches a report or a BIR form. Close enough is not good enough with the BIR.

Plain language, always

No jargon walls. We explain what you owe, why you owe it, and what it means for your business in words you understand.

Proactive, not reactive

We flag deadlines, deductions and cash-flow risks before they become problems — not after.

Ethics over shortcuts

We keep your filings compliant and defensible. Your business deserves books that can stand up to any audit.
