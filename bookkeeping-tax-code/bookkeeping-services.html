Bookkeeping services

Your books, always current — and always making sense

Send us your documents however you like — photos, forwarded emails, bank feeds. We return clear monthly statements and filed BIR returns, on a fixed fee.

What's included

Everything a clean set of books needs

  • Recording of all sales, purchases, expenses and payments
  • Bank, e-wallet, and payment-platform reconciliation (GCash, Maya, Stripe, bank feeds)
  • Monthly profit & loss, balance sheet and cash-flow summary
  • Chart of accounts designed for your industry
  • Supporting schedules for every balance
  • Quarterly BIR business tax returns (2551Q or 2550Q)
  • Secure document collection — snap a photo of receipts from your phone
  • CPA review of every monthly close

Who it's for

Professionals and freelancers — doctors, consultants, designers — who want quarterly and annual taxes handled without drama.

Small businesses — shops, restaurants, agencies, online sellers — that need accurate numbers to make daily decisions.

Corporations that want a reliable finance back office without hiring a full team in-house.

Your monthly rhythm

What a typical month looks like with us

  1. 01

    You send documents (anytime)

    Snap photos of receipts, forward invoice emails, or connect your bank and payment platforms. Most clients spend under 15 minutes a month.

  2. 02

    We record, reconcile and review

    Your bookkeeper records everything, reconciles your accounts, and the supervising CPA reviews the close.

  3. 03

    You receive your statements

    Profit & loss, balance sheet and cash-flow summary — plus a short note on anything that needs your attention.

  4. 04

    Deadlines get filed

    Your quarterly business tax return is prepared and filed ahead of the BIR deadline, with payment instructions sent to you.
