How filing works

A calm, four-step filing season

  1. 01

    Document checklist

    We send you a short, plain-language checklist for your situation — no more wondering what the IRS might need.

  2. 02

    Preparation & review

    We prepare the return, apply every legitimate deduction, and a senior accountant reviews it before anything is filed.

  3. 03

    Filing & payment

    We e-file where possible and send you payment instructions and confirmations.

  4. 04

    Your copies, archived

    You receive filed copies and we keep an archive, so future audits, loans or applications are painless.

What you can legitimately save

Deductions most filers miss

  • Optional standard deduction vs itemized — we compute both and use the lower tax.
  • Home-office and equipment expenses for freelancers and professionals.
  • Car, travel and representation expenses properly documented for businesses.
  • Loss carryovers from prior years, estimated payments already made, and credits you qualify for.
  • Tax relief under CREATE for registered businesses, and incentives you may qualify for.
