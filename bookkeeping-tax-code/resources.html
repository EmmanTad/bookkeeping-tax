Resources · Tax calendar

The BIR deadlines that matter, in one place

A plain-language guide to the recurring filing dates for Philippine businesses and self-employed professionals. Our clients never have to track these — we do.

Every month

Withholding taxes are remitted the month after they are withheld.

10th

1601-C, 0619-E, 0619-F

Employers and payors of income payments

Remit withholding tax on compensation and expanded/final withholding taxes withheld in the previous month (manual filers).

15th

1601-C, 0619-E, 0619-F

eFPS filers

Remittance schedule for filers enrolled in the Electronic Filing and Payment System, based on their assigned group.

Every quarter

Business tax returns follow the calendar quarter; quarterly income tax is due 60 days after each of the first three quarters.

Jan 25, Apr 25, Jul 25, Oct 25

2550Q (VAT) · 2551Q (Percentage tax)

VAT and non-VAT businesses

Quarterly business tax returns for the quarter just ended.

May 15, Aug 15, Nov 15

1701Q · 1702Q

Self-employed professionals; corporations and partnerships

Quarterly income tax returns for the first three quarters of the year.

Every year

The big annual dates — we prepare everything in advance so these never sneak up on you.

January 31

1604-C · BIR 2316

Employers

Annual information return on compensation withheld, and issuance of BIR 2316 certificates to employees.

February 28

1604-F

Payors of income payments

Annual information return on income payments subject to expanded and final withholding taxes.

April 15

1701 · 1702

Individuals, professionals; corporations and partnerships

Annual income tax returns for the prior calendar year — and the fourth-quarter return combined with it.

Good to know

  • Taxes withheld in December are remitted on January 15 of the following year.
  • eFPS filers follow different (usually later) filing windows depending on their group.
  • The BIR occasionally moves deadlines through Revenue Memorandum Circulars — for example, the October 2026 VAT and percentage tax deadline was extended from October 26 to October 29 (RMC 115-2026).
  • Always confirm against the official BIR tax calendar at bir.gov.ph before making payments.
