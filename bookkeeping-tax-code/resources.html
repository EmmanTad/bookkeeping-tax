Resources · Tax calendar

The tax deadlines that matter, in one place

A plain-language guide to the recurring federal and Illinois filing dates for small businesses and self-employed professionals. Our clients never have to track these — we do.

Every month

Many tax payments are due the month after the activity.

20th

Illinois ST-1

Businesses collecting sales tax

Monthly sales tax return and payment for the prior month, for businesses on a monthly filing schedule.

Per schedule

Federal payroll tax deposits

Employers

Federal income tax withholding, Social Security and Medicare are deposited monthly or semiweekly, depending on your deposit schedule.

Every quarter

Payroll returns follow the calendar quarter, and estimated income tax is paid four times a year.

Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31

Form 941 · IL-941

Employers

Quarterly federal and Illinois payroll tax returns for the quarter just ended.

Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15

Form 1040-ES

Self-employed people, partners and S corporation owners

Quarterly estimated income tax payments for the year.

Every year

The big annual dates — we prepare everything in advance so these never sneak up on you.

January 31

W-2 · 1099-NEC

Employers

Wage statements to employees and payment statements to contractors, filed and sent to recipients.

March 15

Form 1065 · Form 1120-S

Partnerships and S corporations

Business returns and owner Schedule K-1s for calendar-year partnerships and S corporations.

April 15

Form 1040 · Form 1120

Self-employed individuals and C corporations

Annual federal and Illinois income tax returns for the prior calendar year.

Good to know

  • When a due date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, it moves to the next business day.
  • An extension to file is not an extension to pay, so estimated payments still matter.
  • The IRS and the Illinois Department of Revenue can move deadlines after disasters or law changes, so dates can change.
  • Always confirm dates at irs.gov and tax.illinois.gov before making payments.

Rather not track deadlines at all?

Our clients get every filing prepared ahead of time, with reminders only when we need something from them.
